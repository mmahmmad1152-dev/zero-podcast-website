# ZERO Podcast — Official Website

The official website of **ZERO Podcast (صِفر)**, an Arabic-first interview podcast from Doha, Qatar, about the human journey behind success — the setbacks, uncertainty, sacrifices, decisions, and turning points that precede every achievement.

## Status

- **Season One is in production.** No episodes, guests, sponsors, or metrics are published yet, and the site says so honestly — nothing on the site is fabricated.
- The nomination and partnership forms validate fully and produce a formatted document the visitor can download or copy, because no receiving inbox is configured yet. This is stated openly on the forms themselves.

## Pages (hash-routed views in a single document)

- **Home** — cinematic opening with the brand's Iconic Reveal film, a four-chapter scroll narrative (العدم، السقوط، نقطة التحوّل، النهوض) with a chapter progress rail, an editorial-paper manifesto band, Season One presented as "Issue 01" with in-production themes, nomination and partnership bands, and a closing statement.
- **Episodes** — the archive's standards (numbering, credits block, full Arabic transcripts) and an honest forthcoming-season state.
- **Our Story** — why ZERO exists, in editorial composition.
- **Guest Nomination** — accessible form: nominator, proposed guest, story significance, contact details.
- **Sponsors & Partnerships** — the editorial concept and collaboration formats, with an inquiry form. No audience metrics or sponsor claims.
- **Contact** — no invented email or social accounts; channels are marked as announced at launch and route to the two forms.

## Design system

- **Canvas:** warm charcoal / near-black, with warm-ivory editorial sections (the brand's approved soft, matte, comfortable direction).
- **Type:** warm ivory on charcoal; muted neutral grey for secondary information.
- **Accent:** the brand red of the ZERO mark's triangle, used as punctuation only; restrained champagne-gold hairlines.
- **Typography (all SIL Open Font License, via Google Fonts):** Noto Kufi Arabic (display), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (body/UI), Amiri (quotations). Arabic line-height 1.8–1.9; letter-spacing is never applied to Arabic.
- **Motion:** one scroll-scrubbed hero sequence only; chapter-based reveals; `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected; no scroll hijacking; content is never dependent on animation.
- **Logo:** the authentic صفر mark (black wordmark, red inverted triangle), alpha-cleaned and used unaltered in header, closing section, and footer.

## Assets note

`index.html` references media assets (the Iconic Reveal film, poster, logo PNGs, and photography) that are served with the hosted live build. Binary media could not be included in this repository snapshot because the upload channel used accepts UTF-8 text only; the assets are preserved with the production build.

## Research foundations

Design research (24 references, read 2026-10-24) included Thmanyah (Arabic-first RTL media), Apple AirPods Pro (scroll-scrubbed film), A24 Films (credits-block art direction), 99% Invisible (episode architecture), Kinfolk (seasons as numbered issues), The Pudding, Monocle, NEOM, and the House of Wonders. Engineering references: GSAP + ScrollTrigger (GreenSock standard licence, free of charge), Lenis smooth scroll (MIT), and the official GSAP image-sequence scrub pattern.
